About Covenant Theological College

A constitutional commitment to historic orthodoxy, rigorous scholarship, and missional leadership development.

History

Founded in 1973, the college has trained ministers, missionaries, and educators across multiple denominations.

Mission & Vision

To form wise and holy leaders who proclaim Christ faithfully in church, academy, and public life.

Statement of Faith

Scripture’s authority, the Trinity, salvation by grace through faith, holiness of life, and the Church’s global mission.

Leadership

President: Dr. Elias Ward · Provost: Rev. Naomi Pierce · Dean of Students: Pastor Judah Kent.

Accreditation

Accredited through recognized regional and faith-based bodies with yearly quality audits.

Affiliations

Partnered with seminaries, mission boards, and Christian counseling networks.
